## Pending
- 2026-10-17 **Meaning of "days" in `INTRADAY_REFRESH_DAYS`** (000-ingest-refresh-window). Chose: the last N *trading* days present in the downloaded data, so a Monday run still refreshes Friday. Alternative: N calendar days back from now (a Monday run with N=2 would refresh nothing from the previous week). The daily window works the same way (last N sessions in the download). To switch, change the `key`/window computation passed to `_refresh_start` in `investbot/data/ingestion/prices.py`.
- 2026-10-17 **Candle `CDL_NET_SCORE` weighting and scale** (`investbot/scoring/technical/candle_patterns.py`).
  Chosen: every pattern counts equally; a confirmed ±200 signal weighs 1.0, plain ±100 and TA-Lib's weaker ±80 (ENGULFING/HARAMI/HARAMICROSS) weigh 0.5; the sum is divided by `NET_SCORE_SCALE = 3.0` and clipped to [-1, 1]. On 20 NIFTY50 daily histories about 2.2% of bars clip.
  Alternatives: (a) a larger scale (e.g. 5.0) so busy bars don't saturate; (b) per-pattern reliability weights from a backtest (e.g. Bulkowski hit rates or our own forward returns); (c) treat ±80 as its own weight (e.g. 0.4) instead of folding it into plain.
  How to switch: change `CONFIRMED_WEIGHT`, `PLAIN_WEIGHT` or `NET_SCORE_SCALE` at the top of the module. (b) needs a weights dict and the backtest layer. Nothing consumes the score yet, so changing it is free until `scorer.py` lands.
- 2026-10-17 **Regime label inputs and thresholds** (`investbot/scoring/technical/regime.py`, 030-regime).
  Chosen: the label uses the 100-bar Hurst and Lyapunov (`REGIME_WINDOW = 100`, which responds within months, not a year). Trend vs range is `sigmoid(0.5·(H−0.5)/0.05 + 0.5·(ADX_14−25)/5)`. Chaos is `sigmoid(0.75·(VOL_RATIO−1.25)/0.2 + 0.25·(LYAP_100−0.30)/0.02)`, where VOL_RATIO = 20-bar vol / 250-bar vol. `REGIME` is the argmax of the three scores and `REGIME_CONFIDENCE` = top score − runner-up, which is continuous and 0 where the label flips. On the 50 NIFTY50 daily histories this gives about 47% range, 50% trend and 3% chaotic. The chaos midpoint (1.25, about the 85th percentile of VOL_RATIO) is set on the cautious side, so stress gets flagged more often, not less.
  Alternatives: (a) `regime_window = 250` for a slower, steadier label; (b) a higher chaos midpoint (1.4 gives about 1.7% chaotic); (c) confidence = the top score itself, rescaled from [1/3, 1], instead of the top-two gap, which never drops to 0; (d) fit the thresholds on forward returns once backtesting exists.
  How to switch: pass `regime_window=` to `compute_regime`, or edit the named constants at the top of the module (`HURST_*`, `ADX_*`, `VOL_RATIO_*`, `LYAP_*`, `*_WEIGHT`). (c) is a one-line change in `regime_scores`. Nothing consumes the regime yet.
- 2026-10-17 **Support/resistance parameter defaults** (020-support-resistance). All of these are module constants in `investbot/scoring/technical/support_resistance.py` and keyword arguments of `compute_support_resistance_columns`, so they can be switched without code changes. Chose: `PIVOT_LEFT_BARS=3` and `PIVOT_RIGHT_BARS=3` (a pivot is known 3 bars late); `SR_MAX_PIVOTS=20` (highs and lows together); `SR_CLUSTER_PCT=0.5` measured from the cluster's lowest price (greedy, so a cluster is never wider than 0.5%); recency weight `0.5**(age/SR_RECENCY_HALFLIFE)` with a 60-bar half-life; `SR_STRENGTH_*` is raw recency-weighted touches, not normalised. `VP_BINS=50`; the VP window includes bar t; `VP_POS` is clipped to ±1 outside the value area, so "just above VAH" and "far above VAH" both read +1. `FIB_NEAREST_DIST_PCT` is signed (close minus level). Alternatives: 5/5 pivots (fewer, slower, more significant levels); strength normalised to a 0–1 share of total weight (comparable across assets); unclipped `VP_POS` (keeps distance information outside the value area); ATR-based instead of percentage cluster tolerance (adapts to volatility).
- 2026-10-17 **Multi-timeframe alignment rules** (`investbot/scoring/technical/multi_timeframe.py`, 040-multi-timeframe).
  Chosen: daily bars become visible at `DAILY_AVAILABLE_AT = 16:00` IST on their date, so every intraday bar on day D (including the 15:15–15:30 bar) uses D-1's daily values. A 1h bar is visible from the 15m bar that closes at the same time. Per-timeframe trend = mean(sign(EMA_20−EMA_50), SUPERTd_10_3.0); momentum regime = +1 if RSI_14 > `RSI_BULL = 55`, −1 if < `RSI_BEAR = 45`, else 0. `MTF_TREND_ALIGN` / `MTF_MOMENTUM_ALIGN` are the equal-weighted (`TIMEFRAME_WEIGHTS = (1, 1, 1)`) mean across 15m/1h/1d, NaN until all three exist. `MTF_REGIME_1D` and `MTF_REGIME_CONFIDENCE_1D` come from `regime.py` on daily bars only (intraday regime is uncalibrated).
  Alternatives: (a) heavier weight on the higher timeframes (e.g. 1/2/3), the classic "trade with the higher-timeframe trend" view; (b) RSI bands 60/40 (fewer, stronger momentum calls) or a continuous `(RSI−50)/20` instead of discrete regimes; (c) a "partial" daily view that lets the final 15m bar of D see a provisional daily bar built from D's own 15m bars (it is not NSE's official close, which is a 15:00–15:30 VWAP published after the close).
  How to switch: the constants at the top of the module, or the `timeframe_weights=` / `daily_available_at=` arguments of `mtf_alignment`. Nothing consumes the MTF columns yet.
- 2026-10-17 **Confluence signal polarity and regime weighting** (`investbot/scoring/technical/confluence.py`, `investbot/config/scoring_weights.py`, 050-confluence).
  Chosen: (1) Oscillators (RSI, Stochastic, Williams %R, CCI, Ultimate, MFI, Bollinger %B, Keltner position) follow momentum: linear from -1 at the lower band through 0 at the midline to +1 at the upper band (RSI 30/50/70), then fade back to `OSC_EXTREME_VALUE = 0.0` at the extreme. RSI 85 reads +0.5 and RSI 100 reads 0, so overbought is treated as exhaustion, not as a sell signal. Mean reversion in range regimes comes from upweighting momentum and S/R, where `SR_POSITION` reads +1 on support and -1 at resistance. (2) Family weights are blended `c·W[REGIME] + (1−c)·W[neutral]`, c = REGIME_CONFIDENCE. While the regime is still warming up (first ~251 daily bars), neutral weights are used. (3) A chaotic regime multiplies TECH_SCORE by `REGIME_SCORE_MULTIPLIER["chaotic"] = 0.5`. (4) TECH_SCORE is NaN until every family in the frame is out of warmup (~200 daily bars, set by EMA_200). No partial family averages. (5) CDL3LINESTRIKE keeps TA-Lib's sign.
  Alternatives: (a) `OSC_EXTREME_VALUE = -1` (full mean reversion: a pinned oscillator is a sell signal), or `+1` (pure momentum, no fade); (b) regime-dependent polarity (follow momentum in trend, revert in range), which needs two forms per oscillator; (c) blend the weights with the soft `REGIME_SCORE_*` instead of label + confidence; (d) chaotic multiplier 1.0 (no shrink) or lower (e.g. 0.25, near flat in stress); (e) allow partial family averages during warmup so the score starts earlier.
  How to switch: (a) the `OSC_EXTREME_VALUE` constant, or pass `extreme=` per signal in `SIGNALS`; (c) one function, `family_weights`; (d) `REGIME_SCORE_MULTIPLIER` in `config/scoring_weights.py`; (e) `_evaluate` (use `np.nanmean` and drop the `complete` mask). Nothing consumes TECH_SCORE yet.
- 2026-10-17 **Technical scorer storage, lookback and as_of rules** (`investbot/scoring/technical/scorer.py`, `investbot/data/models.py`, 060-scorer).
  Chosen: (1) a new `technical_scores` table (one row per asset, timeframe and bar) keeps TECH_SCORE on its native [-1, 1] scale with the per-family scores, regime, reasons and data-quality flags. The plan's `scores` table (0-100 sub-scores) is left to the combined layer, which can map `(s + 1) * 50`. (2) Fixed lookback: the last `DAILY_LOOKBACK_BARS = 750` daily bars and `INTRADAY_LOOKBACK_BARS = 1600` 15m bars at or before as_of, so a score doesn't depend on how much older history the DB holds (on RELIANCE.NS, 750 bars match the full 5 years to ~1e-5). (3) `as_of` as a datetime: a daily bar of date D counts from D 16:00 IST (same as `multi_timeframe.DAILY_AVAILABLE_AT`), a 15m bar from its close; a plain date includes that whole day. (4) Intraday scoring uses the *daily* regime (MTF_REGIME_1D) for the confluence weights, since regime.py is uncalibrated on 15m bars. (5) Stale = last bar more than `STALE_AFTER_BUSINESS_DAYS = 3` business days before the reference time; a flag only, the score is still returned. (6) Compute errors are flagged and logged, never persisted or cached.
  Alternatives: (1) write straight into `scores.technical_score` on 0-100 and skip the new table (loses the family breakdown); (2) load the full history (slower, scores drift slightly as history grows) or a shorter window like 500 bars (drifts ~2e-4 on RELIANCE); (3) treat a datetime as_of's own date as known (lookahead before 16:00); (4) compute a 15m regime once it is calibrated; (5) a stricter threshold (1 day) or holding back the score when stale.
  How to switch: (1) a mapping in the combined layer; (2)/(5) the module constants at the top of `scorer.py`; (3) `_cutoffs`; (4) `compute_intraday_frame`. Bump `SCORER_VERSION` after any change so cached rows are recomputed.

They are not real sessions, and they would inject fake zero-range bars into ATR and the patterns. Backup before purge: `~/investbot_data/investbot.db.pre-dq-purge-20261017`.
- 2026-10-17 **Stale recent bars**: upsert a trailing window (5 daily sessions, 2 intraday days) and never store today's daily bar before 16:00 IST. This is task 000-ingest-refresh-window.
