## Pending

- 2026-10-18 **Holding-period rule** (task 110-transaction-costs-lots). Section 2(42A): "held for more than twelve months". Chosen: calendar months (`ltcg_holding_days // 365 * 12`), and the first long-term sale date is the day *after* the calendar anniversary (a 29 Feb buy has its anniversary on 28 Feb, so 1 Mar). Alternatives: (a) a sale *on* the anniversary already counts as long-term; (b) a plain `>= 365` days count, as in the master plan's SQL (it flips a day early or late around leap years and differs from the law's month count). Chosen option is the conservative one (never treats a gain as long-term early). To switch to (a) set `LTCG_STRICTLY_AFTER_ANNIVERSARY = False` in `investbot/trading/tax_rules.py`; (b) needs a code change. The master plan's `>= 365 days` SQL should be aligned with `is_long_term`.
- 2026-10-18 **FIFO only vs. specific identification** (task 110-transaction-costs-lots). Master plan §2.8 4e says to pick lots to minimise tax (specific identification). CBDT Circular 768 (1998) mandates FIFO for shares held in demat form, for both the holding period and the cost. Chosen: `sell_fifo` is the only disposal method and there is no tax-optimal lot picking. Under FIFO the tax levers are *how much* and *when* to sell, not *which lots*, so the tax filter (§2.8 4a/4b) should reason at position level using `unrealised_by_lot`. Alternative: implement specific identification if you get advice that it is valid for our holdings (it would be a new function; `sell_fifo` stays). Plan §2.8 4e and build-order item 13 ("select optimal lots on sell") should be rewritten.
- 2026-10-18 **Transfer expenses on crypto (FLAT term)** (task 110-transaction-costs-lots). Section 115BBH disallows any deduction except cost of acquisition for VDA income, so `sell_fifo` sets `sell_costs = 0` for `FLAT` realisations even though the task formula would deduct them. Chosen: no deduction (conservative, higher estimated tax). Alternative: deduct `total - stt` like equity. To switch set `FLAT_TERM_DEDUCTS_SELL_COSTS = True` in `investbot/trading/lot_tracker.py`. Related: `estimate_costs` charges the equity-style flat ₹20 brokerage on crypto too, because the crypto regime has no brokerage field. Real crypto exchange fees are percentage-based; revisit when the crypto paper engine is built.
- 2026-10-18 **Tax ledger: rate-regime split inside one FY** (task 120-tax-ledger). FY 2024-25 has two rate regimes (changed 23 July 2024). Chosen: gains are bucketed by (term, rate at their sell date); losses and the LTCG exemption are applied to the **higher-rate bucket first** (taxpayer-optimal, lowest tax). Alternatives: pro rata across buckets, or the older bucket first (higher tax, closer to a strict reading if the law prescribes an order). To switch, change the sort in `_absorb` in `investbot/trading/tax_ledger.py`. Only FY 2024-25 is affected; other years have one rate.
- 2026-10-18 **Tax ledger: exemption amount** (task 120-tax-ledger). The annual LTCG exemption uses the regime in force on the FY's **last day (31 March)**: ₹1L for FY 2023-24 and earlier, ₹1.25L from FY 2024-25 (the whole year gets ₹1.25L, as the law gives one limit per assessment year). Alternative: look the exemption up by the date of the first or last sale, which would give ₹1L for sales before 23 July 2024. No parameter; it is one line (`fy_end_regime`) in `compute_fiscal_year_tax`.
- 2026-10-18 **Tax ledger: crypto before 2022-04-01** (task 120-tax-ledger). `regime_for("crypto", d)` raises for dates before the 115BBH regime starts, so a crypto backtest from 2021 fails loudly instead of silently taxing at 0% or 30%. Alternative: add a pre-2022 crypto regime row (flat 0%, losses usable? needs your call on how to treat pre-VDA gains).
- 2026-10-18 **Risk parity = equal risk contribution, not inverse volatility** (task 130-risk-covariance-parity). Plan §2.7 says "equalising risk contribution", §6 and the build order say "inverse-volatility". Chosen: `risk_parity_weights` solves long-only ERC (Spinu 2013, Newton), because inverse-vol only equals ERC when all pairwise correlations are equal; with correlated NIFTY sectors (banks) inverse-vol overweights the correlated cluster's risk. Alternative: use `inverse_volatility_weights` (same module) as the risk-parity leg; it is one call to swap in the allocator (task 140). On the scratch DB (51 assets, as of 2026-06-30) ERC weights span 0.96%–4.1%.
- 2026-10-18 **Covariance window and history requirement** (task 130-risk-covariance-parity). Chosen: last `COV_WINDOW = 250` daily returns, an asset needs `COV_MIN_OBS = 120` non-NaN returns in that window and the estimate needs 120 complete rows (otherwise the asset with the most NaNs is dropped, logged in `CovEstimate.excluded`). Shrinkage: Ledoit-Wolf 2004 towards constant correlation, no alternatives offered. Excluded assets get no risk-parity weight at all; the allocator (task 140) must decide what to do with them (hold at 0, or carry the BL weight only). To change, pass `window=` / `min_obs=` or edit the constants in `investbot/trading/risk.py`.

- 2026-10-18 **Evaluation cost per round trip** (task 100-eval-harness). The task says ≈ 0.25%, but the exact rates in `config/tax_regimes.py` (via `round_trip_cost_pct`) already give 0.270% on a ₹1 lakh order (₹20 + ₹20 brokerage is 0.04% of it) before any slippage. - 2026-10-18 **Quintiles of discrete signals** (task 100-eval-harness). Ties share a bucket (average ranks), so a ±1 signal fills only some quintiles and Q5/Q1 are often empty. Chosen: "top" and "bottom" are the highest and lowest *non-empty* buckets of each date (identical to Q5/Q1 for continuous signals); the top-bucket portfolio, turnover and cost check use the same top bucket. Alternatives: strict Q5−Q1 (NaN for discrete signals), or random tie-breaking (noisy, needs a seed). Switch in `top_bottom` in `investbot/evaluation/metrics.py`.
- 2026-10-18 **"Promising" rule details** (task 100-eval-harness). BH runs over every signal × horizon on the OOS next-open rows (123 tests), q < 0.10, and the sign of each test block's mean IC must equal the sign of the OOS mean IC in ≥ 70% of blocks (blocks without data are left out of the share). A significant **negative** IC is also flagged promising, with `direction = "inverted"`: nothing is flipped, but it is evidence for a polarity change. Alternative: only count the designed polarity. Constants `PROMISING_Q`, `PROMISING_SIGN_SHARE` in `investbot/evaluation/evaluate.py`. Other harness constants: `MIN_TRAIN_BARS = 252` (first test block after one trading year of usable TECH_SCORE plus the embargo), test blocks are calendar half-years, `MIN_NAMES = 10` names per date.

- 2026-10-18 **Corporate actions: an ex-dated table instead of a periodic `adj_close` re-download** (task 190-corporate-actions). Chosen: store dividends and splits in `corporate_actions` by ex-date, captured from the daily download we already make (`actions=True`, no extra requests). Why: it is point-in-time (a row is known from its ex-date, so total-return labels in task 200 can't leak), it costs no extra yfinance calls, and the split rows let `detect_unadjusted_splits` / `python main.py dq` find history that was never re-adjusted (price rows are insert-only, so a split after the first ingest leaves older bars at the old scale). Alternative: periodically re-download the full history and refresh `adj_close` (one request per asset per refresh, hits the rate limits, gives no ex-dates and no split detection, and between refreshes the factors are stale). Notes: yfinance reports Indian **bonus issues as splits** (a 1:1 bonus is factor 2.0), and its **dividend amounts are adjusted for later splits**, so the stored value is per current share and is re-stated (updated in place) after a split. Ex-dates before the first stored bar are credited to the first bar by `dividends_on_bars`. Detection tolerance is `tol = 0.15` (relative) in `detect_unadjusted_splits`; a split with factor within ~15% of 1 can't be told apart from a gap. `dq` only reports; re-adjusting history is left to you (e.g. delete that asset's `price_daily` rows and re-ingest).
