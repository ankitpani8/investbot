## Cost reality check: top-quintile long-only, open→close per period (OOS)

Equal weight, rebalanced every h bars; net = gross − turnover × 0.320%. Universe = equal weight over all names in the same periods. Simple annualisation (mean period return × 252 / h); not compounded, no taxes. Each period runs from open[t+1] to close[t+h], so the overnight gap before the next entry is not held: absolute returns at short horizons are mostly the (negative) open-to-close drift. Compare against the universe column, not against zero.

## Held portfolio (open-to-open, compounded)

The same top-quintile equal-weight portfolio, rebalanced every h bars, but held continuously: each period runs open[t+1] → open[t+1+h], so consecutive periods tile the OOS timeline and the overnight gap is included. Net = gross − turnover × 0.320% per period, compounded. CAGR over the invested periods (n × h / 252 years); vol = sd of net period returns × √(252 / h); max DD = worst peak-to-trough of the compounded net curve; IR = mean / sd of (net − universe) period returns × √(252 / h). No risk-free rate: the benchmark is the equal-weight universe over the same periods. The OOS blocks are contiguous, so the curve spans the whole OOS window. No taxes.

## Caveats

- **Universe: point-in-time NIFTY50.** A stock is scored only on dates when it was an index member (`index_membership`, changes effective on or before the date); names that left the index count for the periods they were members. OOS coverage (members with a TECH_SCORE / members, per date): mean 95.3%, minimum 90.0% (2026-09-30). **Coverage is below 100%:** members without usable price history (or still in TECH_SCORE warmup) are left out on those dates, so a residual survivorship bias remains (no usable prices during the OOS window: BSE.NS, ETERNAL.NS, INDIGO.NS, ITCHOTELS.NS, JIOFIN.NS, KWIL.NS, LTIM.NS, MAXHEALTH.NS, TMCV.NS, UPL.NS).
- Five years of daily history leave about three years out of sample after warmup (EMA_200, regime window) and the first training year; ~50 names per date.
- A missing session (purged bar) is skipped, not filled: t+1 is the next *stored* bar.
- A signal significant only with its sign inverted is a finding about polarity, not something to switch on: polarity decisions go to Ankit with this evidence.
