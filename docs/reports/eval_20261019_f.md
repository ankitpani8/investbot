## Caveats

- **Universe: point-in-time NIFTY50.** A stock is scored only on dates when it was an index member (`index_membership`, changes effective on or before the date); names that left the index count for the periods they were members. OOS coverage (members with a TECH_SCORE / members, per date): mean 95.3%, minimum 90.0% (2026-09-30). **Coverage is below 100%:** members without usable price history (or still in TECH_SCORE warmup) are left out on those dates, so a residual survivorship bias remains (no usable prices during the OOS window: BSE.NS, ETERNAL.NS, INDIGO.NS, ITCHOTELS.NS, JIOFIN.NS, KWIL.NS, LTIM.NS, MAXHEALTH.NS, TMCV.NS, UPL.NS).
- Five years of daily history leave about three years out of sample after warmup (EMA_200, regime window) and the first training year; ~50 names per date.
- A missing session (purged bar) is skipped, not filled: t+1 is the next *stored* bar.
- Total return credits gross cash dividends; dividend tax (slab rate) is not modelled, and neither is STCG/LTCG on the trades.
- A signal significant only with its sign inverted is a finding about polarity, not something to switch on: polarity decisions go to Ankit with this evidence.
