# Regime study 2026-10-19: real-time regime label vs the ex-post regime

> **Universe: point-in-time NIFTY50.** A stock's regime features count only on dates when it was an index member (`index_membership`, changes effective on or before the date); names that left the index count for the periods they were members. OOS coverage (members with a finite REGIME / members, per date): mean 95.3%, minimum 90.0% (2026-09-30). **Coverage is below 100%:** members without usable price history (or still in REGIME warmup) are left out on those dates, so a residual survivorship bias remains (no usable prices during the OOS window: BSE.NS, ETERNAL.NS, INDIGO.NS, ITCHOTELS.NS, JIOFIN.NS, KWIL.NS, LTIM.NS, MAXHEALTH.NS, TMCV.NS, UPL.NS).

Generated by `python -m scripts.regime_study` (`investbot/evaluation/regime_study.py`). A study only: no threshold, span or weight is changed, and nothing here recommends new constants. Hypotheses, expected signs, horizons, spans and the null seed were pre-registered in the module docstring before the first run.

## Setup

- Universe: point-in-time NIFTY50 from `index_membership`: 71 names were members at some point up to the last bar, 51 of them have price rows and 51 have ≥ 251 bars and are studied; 62977 daily bars, 2021-10-18 → 2026-10-16. The conditioning features (REGIME, the confidence spans, REGIME_SCORE_TREND/CHAOS, HURST_100, VOL_RATIO) are computed on each asset's full stored history and set to NaN on the dates when it was not a member, on the real and the shuffled-null panel alike (58764 member bars of 62977). The ex-post targets are never masked: a target window may run past an exit, as the harness labels do.
- Coverage on OOS dates (members with a finite REGIME / members): mean 95.3%, minimum 90.0% on 2026-09-30, over the OOS dates with ≥ 10 covered members (1 OOS dates with fewer are left out).
- Members with **no price rows** (never filled; OOS member days in brackets): BSE.NS (12), ETERNAL.NS (383), GAIL.NS (0), HDFC.NS (0), HINDPETRO.NS (0), IBULHSGFIN.NS (0), INDIGO.NS (258), INFRATEL.NS (0), IOC.NS (0), ITCHOTELS.NS (26), JIOFIN.NS (383), KWIL.NS (54), LTIM.NS (183), MAXHEALTH.NS (258), SHREECEM.NS (0), TMCV.NS (22), UPL.NS (59), VEDL.NS (0), YESBANK.NS (0), ZEEL.NS (0).
- Membership before 2019-03-29 is not sourced and falls back to today's active stocks: 0 calendar dates (0 OOS).
- Comparison universe "active": the 45 active stocks with ≥ 251 bars, unmasked (the pre-260 study), on the same walk-forward splits; see "Universe effect".
- Out-of-sample (OOS): 6 walk-forward test blocks of 6 months, 2024-01-01 → 2026-10-16, 690 dates; same settings as the harness (≥ 252 bars after the first date with REGIME on ≥ 10 names, embargo 20 bars). Blocks: `2024-01-01..2024-06-28`, `2024-07-01..2024-12-31`, `2025-01-01..2025-06-30`, `2025-07-01..2025-12-31`, `2026-01-01..2026-06-30`, `2026-07-01..2026-10-16`. Every statistic below uses OOS dates only. Targets near the end of the data are NaN (their window runs past the last bar), so the last block is shorter for H = 60 and FWD_HURST_100.
- Conditioning: `regime.compute_regime` with the production constants (REGIME_WINDOW 100, CONFIDENCE_EMA_SPAN 5); confidence spans 1, 3, 5, 10, 20 via `regime.smooth_confidence` (span 1 = REGIME_CONFIDENCE_RAW).
- Targets (labels, closes t … t+H): FWD_ER, FWD_AC1, FWD_VR5, FWD_VOLX (std of forward returns / REALVOL_250[t]), FWD_MDD at H ∈ {20, 60}; FWD_HURST_100 = DFA Hurst of the next 100 returns (same function and window as the real-time HURST_100).
- Null: every asset's bars shuffled in time (`shuffled_null_bars`, seed 20261018): returns, bar shapes and volumes kept, all serial dependence removed.
- Tests: 35 pre-registered tests (conditional means, rank association, calibration), Newey-West t with lag H, two-sided normal p, Benjamini–Hochberg over all of them. **Supported** = expected sign, BH q < 0.10 and the expected sign in ≥ 70% of the test blocks; **wrong sign** = opposite sign with q < 0.10.
- SCORER_VERSION 4; git `068e66b`; DB: max price date 2026-10-16, 62977 price rows, 0 corporate-action rows; runtime 61 s (1 s of it for the active-universe comparison); peak RSS 182 MB.

## Verdict per pre-registered hypothesis

**H1 persistence.** Bars labelled trend are followed by more persistent paths than bars labelled range (trend − range > 0). ## Caveats

- Residual survivorship: members without price rows (or with too few bars) are left out on their member dates (see Setup); the coverage note at the top gives the share of members studied. Before 2019-03-29 membership falls back to today's active stocks.
- The targets overlap heavily (consecutive dates share H − 1 bars, 99 for FWD_HURST_100), so the effective sample is far smaller than the number of days; the Newey-West t accounts for this only approximately, especially for H = 60 and 100 bars against ~690 OOS dates.
- The null shares one date permutation across assets with the same calendar, so its cross-section still co-moves; only serial dependence is removed.
- FWD_VOLX is normalised by REALVOL_250 and VOL_RATIO = REALVOL_20 / REALVOL_250. The shared denominator alone makes VOL_RATIO vs FWD_VOLX positive across names (see the null IC), and volatility clustering adds to it; REALVOL_20 is also inside REALVOL_250's window, so a high VOL_RATIO inflates the normaliser and lowers FWD_VOLX (see the null chaos − rest difference).
- FWD_HURST_100 has the same DFA small-sample bias as the real-time HURST_100; the trend − range difference is unaffected by a common bias.
