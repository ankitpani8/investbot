# Evaluation report 2026-10-18

> **Survivorship bias.** The universe is *today's* NIFTY50 constituents, back-filled over the whole history. Names that were dropped from the index (usually after underperforming) are missing and names that were added (usually after outperforming) are present before they joined. Absolute returns, and any signal correlated with past winners, are biased upward. Historical constituents are an open data task in docs/BACKLOG.md.

Generated by `python main.py evaluate` (`investbot/evaluation/`). Nothing is fitted: every signal is evaluated with its designed polarity on walk-forward test blocks only.

## Setup

- Universe: 51 active stocks; bars 2021-10-18 → 2026-10-16.
- Out-of-sample (OOS): 6 test blocks of 6 months, 2024-01-01 → 2026-10-16, 690 dates. Expanding train window (≥ 252 bars from the first date with TECH_SCORE on ≥ 10 names), embargo of 20 bars before each block.
- Labels: `FWD_RET_h = close[t+h] / open[t+1] − 1` (signal known after t's close, entry at the next open), h ∈ {1, 5, 20}. Close-to-close shown for comparison only.
- IC: daily cross-sectional Spearman; ICIR = mean / sd of daily IC (not annualised); t-stats use Newey-West standard errors with lag h − 1.
- Multiple testing: Benjamini–Hochberg over all 123 signal × horizon tests. **Promising** = BH q < 0.10 and the IC sign holds in ≥ 70% of test blocks.
- Costs: round trip 0.320% of traded value = statutory charges and brokerage from `config/tax_regimes.py` on a ₹1 lakh order (0.270%) + slippage 0.025% per side.
- Runtime: 65 s.

## Caveats

- **Survivorship bias.** The universe is *today's* NIFTY50 constituents, back-filled over the whole history. Names that were dropped from the index (usually after underperforming) are missing and names that were added (usually after outperforming) are present before they joined. Absolute returns, and any signal correlated with past winners, are biased upward. Historical constituents are an open data task in docs/BACKLOG.md.
- Five years of daily history leave about three years out of sample after warmup (EMA_200, regime window) and the first training year; ~50 names per date.
- A missing session (purged bar) is skipped, not filled: t+1 is the next *stored* bar.
- A signal significant only with its sign inverted is a finding about polarity, not something to switch on: polarity decisions go to Ankit with this evidence.
