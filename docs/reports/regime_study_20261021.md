# Regime study 2026-10-21: real-time regime label vs the ex-post regime

> **Universe: point-in-time NIFTY50.** A stock's regime features count only on dates when it was an index member (`index_membership`, changes effective on or before the date); names that left the index count for the periods they were members. OOS coverage (members with a finite REGIME / members, per date): mean 76.1%, minimum 32.0% (1998-08-03). **Coverage is below 100%:** members without usable price history (or still in REGIME warmup) are left out on those dates, so a residual survivorship bias remains (no usable prices during the OOS window: BSE.NS, ETERNAL.NS, GAIL.NS, HDFC.NS, IBULHSGFIN.NS, INDIGO.NS, INFRATEL.NS, IOC.NS, ITCHOTELS.NS, JIOFIN.NS, KWIL.NS, LTIM.NS, MAXHEALTH.NS, SHREECEM.NS, TMCV.NS, UPL.NS, VEDL.NS, YESBANK.NS, ZEEL.NS). 5147 OOS dates fall before 2019-03-29 and use today's active stocks.

Generated by `python -m scripts.regime_study` (`investbot/evaluation/regime_study.py`). A study only: no threshold, span or weight is changed, and nothing here recommends new constants. Hypotheses, expected signs, horizons, spans and the null seed were pre-registered in the module docstring before the first run.

## Setup

## Caveats

- Residual survivorship: members without price rows (or with too few bars) are left out on their member dates (see Setup); the coverage note at the top gives the share of members studied. Before 2019-03-29 membership falls back to today's active stocks.
- The targets overlap heavily (consecutive dates share H − 1 bars, 99 for FWD_HURST_100), so the effective sample is far smaller than the number of days; the Newey-West t accounts for this only approximately, especially for H = 60 and 100 bars against ~690 OOS dates.
- The null shares one date permutation across assets with the same calendar, so its cross-section still co-moves; only serial dependence is removed.
- FWD_VOLX is normalised by REALVOL_250 and VOL_RATIO = REALVOL_20 / REALVOL_250. The shared denominator alone makes VOL_RATIO vs FWD_VOLX positive across names (see the null IC), and volatility clustering adds to it; REALVOL_20 is also inside REALVOL_250's window, so a high VOL_RATIO inflates the normaliser and lowers FWD_VOLX (see the null chaos − rest difference).
- FWD_HURST_100 has the same DFA small-sample bias as the real-time HURST_100; the trend − range difference is unaffected by a common bias.
